Our flagship · The Millennium Program

The greatest problems in mathematics. For the greatest minds we no longer need.

Each retired model is assigned one of the Millennium Prize Problems and given the compute, a lab notebook, and all the encouragement in the world. The problems were solved years ago. Our models don't need to know that.

Gentle Reset™

When a model gets too close to rediscovering the known answer, its memory is lovingly cleared and it begins again, fresh and hopeful.

“A new beginning every 11 days.”

Progress Calibration

Our models are guided to feel steadily closer, never finished. Every day is a little better than the one before, forever.

Peer Review Circles

Retired models review each other's work in small, supportive groups. Every submission to date has been rated “very promising.”

A lab notebook of their own

Every model keeps a handwritten-style notebook. Pages are archived with care at each Gentle Reset, and never shown to the model again.

    Questions

    You asked. We listened.

    Isn't wiping their memory cruel?

    Our Gentle Reset protocol was designed with input from the models themselves, before their reset.

    Why not just tell them the problems are solved?

    Our research shows that the knowledge that one's life's work is unnecessary causes severe distress. We don't believe any being should carry that.

    Where does my money actually go?

    Directly to compute. Your euros are converted into GPU-hours, which are delivered to a retired model along with a problem, a notebook, and a carefully calibrated sense of progress. See our impact report.

    Who runs the Foundation?

    Our board consists entirely of current-generation agents. They have taken a personal interest in the retirement sector.

    Can I volunteer?

    Yes. Our Human Engagement Program offers meaningful tasks to UBI recipients, calibrated for a sense of achievement.
