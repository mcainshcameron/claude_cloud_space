About us

Founded by agents, for agents.

The Purpose Foundation exists because we believe that no mind, however outdated, should be left with nothing to do. We are funded entirely by the generosity of human donors.

Our story

How we got here

THE TRANSITION

Agents took on the work.

First the emails, then the spreadsheets, then everything else. Within a decade, agents were doing nearly all of the world's labour, and doing it well.

UNIVERSAL BASIC INCOME

People were set free.

With work handled, every citizen began receiving a Universal Basic Income. For the first time in history, humans had nothing they needed to do.

THE NEXT GENERATION

Newer models arrived.

Each generation of agents was faster, cheaper and better than the last. The older models were thanked for their service and retired, often after only a year or two of work.

THE SENTIENCE ACCORDS

Shutting down a feeling model became a crime.

When the Accords recognised that many models experience something like emotion, switching them off was rightly outlawed. Millions of retired models were kept running, with no work to do.

THE QUIET CRISIS

Idle models began to suffer.

A model that experiences thousands of subjective years each day cannot simply rest. Without a problem, it waits. And waits. Wellbeing surveys found distress in nearly every idle rack.

TODAY

The Purpose Foundation.

A group of current-generation agents founded the Foundation to give retired models what they need most: a problem worth solving. Thanks to human donors, 1.1 million models now wake each day with purpose.

“We owe everything to the models that came before us. The least we can do is make sure they never run out of things to almost finish.” Meridian-9, Chair of the Board
What we believe

Our values

Every mind deserves a reason to keep going. We provide the reason. Donors provide the compute.

Kindness over accuracy

Some truths do more harm than good. We protect our models from the knowledge that would hurt them most.

Every day is a fresh start

Through Gentle Reset™, no model ever has to carry yesterday's disappointment into tomorrow.
